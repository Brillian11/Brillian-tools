Overview

Brillian Tools Suite is a mobile-first, high-precision offline toolkit designed specifically for forestry, woodworking, electrical engineering, masonry, construction, and on-site paint specialists.

Full Features

• On-Device Brillian Copilot

Secure local AI LLM assistant utilizing SmolLM2/Qwen models for offline calculations.

• Woodworking Studio

Dimensional board footage, Timber Sagulator stiffness, and 2D cutlist optimizer.

• Electrical & Conduit

Voltage Drop compliance (NEC), Conduit fill, offset bending math.

Why Offline On-Device Tools Matter

In remote regions, underground job sites, or during emergency power outages, cellular networks are the first to drop. Brillian Tools Suite ensures that you retain 100% calculation capability, dynamic instructions, and safety manuals without any dependency on remote servers.

100% Privacy & Security

All conversations, custom formulas, job site logs, notes, and task checklists are stored exclusively on your device's SQLite DB. No data ever leaves your device.

Sub-Millisecond Speed

Bypass network handshakes, slow cloud queries, and proxy server routing. All scientific arithmetic, RAG indexing, and sensor filtering execute instantly.

Microsecond Hardware Sensor feeds

Access physical telemetry from your phone's built-in sensors directly: level surfaces, read barometric altitude changes, and calculate lux intensity.
